👟 Sportswear Industry

  • Under Armour's revenue slide continues — Under Armour reported fiscal second-quarter results Friday showing revenue down 7% to about $1.2 billion for the quarter ended Sept. 30, with North America off 7% to $737 million and footwear sales down 11%. CEO Kevin Plank said the company exceeded its operating income outlook even as it flagged further North American declines, in the low double digits, for the current quarter. (SEC filing)
  • Performance footwear still driving growth — Market researcher Circana reported Monday that performance footwear remained the U.S. footwear industry's primary growth engine in the third quarter, even as sport-lifestyle sneakers kept the largest share of sales. A companion Circana holiday-intentions survey found consumers planning to spend selectively rather than pull back, with purchases clustering around Black Friday, Cyber Monday and the final weeks of the season. (Circana)
  • Ecco shutters Portugal and Vietnam plants — Danish footwear maker Ecco announced Monday it is closing its factories in Portugal and Vietnam as part of a sweeping restructuring, idling roughly 650 workers in Santa Maria da Feira and as many as 1,400 in Vietnam, with remaining production consolidated in Indonesia, Thailand and China. The closures came days after CEO Thomas Gøgsig's resignation and are expected to save the company about €80 million a year. (Modaes)

🤝 Community Impact & CSR

  • Willamette Week's Give!Guide kicks off — Portland's largest annual nonprofit fundraising campaign launched Nov. 1, with Willamette Week welcoming readers to its 2026 Give!Guide on Nov. 4: 281 local nonprofits are taking part this year, with organizers aiming for roughly $9 million from at least 17,000 donors and $578,100 in matching funds already pledged. Last year's campaign raised a record $9.26 million for 277 Portland-area nonprofits. (Willamette Week)

🌲 Portland

  • Portlanders vote under ranked choice again — Portland and Multnomah County held their general election Nov. 3, with voters choosing three City Council members each in Districts 3 and 4 along with county offices including sheriff and chair. Because both the city and county use ranked-choice voting, official tabulation continues through sequential rounds at rcvresults.multco.us, with certification not due until Nov. 30. (Multnomah County)
  • PPS families launch letter-writing push — With Portland Public Schools' superintendent set to deliver her final closure recommendation to the board later this month, families launched a letter-writing campaign Oct. 31 urging the board to reconsider or delay the plan. Eleven of the district's 13 dual-language immersion schools are among those on the closure list, part of proposals to shutter as many as 14 schools amid a $65 million budget gap. (KPTV)
